# Comscore

> Comscore is a cross-platform media measurement and audience analytics company that provides a trusted currency for planning, transacting, and evaluating media across digital, social, television, streaming/CTV, and programmatic channels. It publishes first-party audience-measurement SDKs but does not expose a public REST API.

## Company
- [Comscore](https://www.comscore.com): Cross-platform measurement company.
- [About](https://www.comscore.com/About): Company overview.
- [Products](https://www.comscore.com/Products): Advertising, CTV, Digital, Movies, Programmatic Targeting (Proximic), Social, and Television measurement solutions.
- [Insights / Blog](https://www.comscore.com/Insights/Blog): Research and industry insights.
- [Press Room](https://www.comscore.com/About/Press-Room): News and announcements.

## Measurement SDKs
- [ComScore-Android](https://github.com/comscore/ComScore-Android): Audience measurement library for Android.
- [ComScore-iOS-watchOS-tvOS](https://github.com/comscore/ComScore-iOS-watchOS-tvOS): Audience measurement library for iOS, iPadOS, and tvOS.
- [Comscore-Swift-Package-Manager](https://github.com/comscore/Comscore-Swift-Package-Manager): Swift Package Manager distribution of the measurement library.
- [Comscore-Flutter](https://github.com/comscore/Comscore-Flutter): Flutter plugin for audience measurement.
